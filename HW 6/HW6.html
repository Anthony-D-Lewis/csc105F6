My Japanese Study Page

A few ways I am learning Japanese!

Why I Want to Study Japanese

I chose to study Japanese because I have a strong interest in the culture and it is something that will be good for my intelligence.

My end goal with this is to hopefully become fluent within the next 4 years.

Main Categories to Study

  • Hiragana and katakana
  • Vocabulary/Kanji
  • Grammar
  • Input and Output

My Study Routine

  1. Do my daily ANKI review and 12 new flashcards.
  2. Listen to 1 episode of a grammar podcast
  3. Watch some Japanese Youtube videos or anime
  4. Repeat the phrases I hear and learn per day

I would recommend to study for around 1-2 hours daily. But consistency is more important than time studied.

Keep Practicing

Being consistant and making sure im putting in the work each day is key. Even if some days i can only do my anki, its still good do it everyday.
